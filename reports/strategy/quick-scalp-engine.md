# QuickScalp Engine

QuickScalp is a new high-selectivity strategy for 0.4%-1.2% moves, target 15-60 minute holds, aggressive TP1 and fast profit lock.

Inputs: volume expansion, liquidity, EMA alignment, momentum acceleration, ATR expansion, market structure, impulse quality, spread quality.
Rejects: dead markets, low volume, overextended moves, weak invalidation, late entries.
Outputs: entry zone, SL, TP1, TP2, confidence, opportunity/arbitration score fields, and invalidation condition.
