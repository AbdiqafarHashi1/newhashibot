# Strategy Personality Map

- MicroScalper: frequent, fast, quick TP1, profit lock after TP1, only 5m/15m.
- CompressionBreakout: compression/breakout/expansion specialist; requires volume and expansion confirmation.
- SwingContinuation: fewer trades, trend/reclaim focused, larger targets, longer holds, lower expiry tolerance.
- QuickScalp: fastest, most selective, shortest holds, fastest protection activation, rejects late impulse entries.
- Forex strategies: conservative derivatives of crypto personalities with provider/session/spread constraints.
