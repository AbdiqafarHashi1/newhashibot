## Risk-Scaled Capital Projection Method
- Projection is linear R scaling after strategy-edge metrics are known: total return % ≈ `totalR * risk_per_trade`; max drawdown % ≈ `maxDD_R * risk_per_trade`.
- Example: +50R projects to about +25%, +50%, and +100% at 0.5%, 1%, and 2% risk/trade respectively, while 15R max drawdown projects to about 7.5%, 15%, and 30%.
- These are not guarantees and are not optimizer targets. They are leverage/risk illustrations only.
