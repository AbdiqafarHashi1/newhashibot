**Size the loss before choosing leverage.** Risk is the cash lost if the stop is hit, as a percentage of account equity. Leverage changes margin requirements; it does not make an oversized position safer. Include spread, fees and possible slippage in your sizing. Never move a stop farther away to keep a losing trade open.

• Standard Account: Risk 0.5%–1.0% max per trade (Max 3 open trades total; max 1 per symbol)
• Prop Firm Account: Risk 0.25%–0.50% max per trade (Max 2 open trades total; max 1 per symbol)
• Never stack same-direction trades or over-leverage.

One idea per symbol. Do not open several BTC positions because several strategies agree. Do not treat highly correlated trades as independent: long EURUSD plus long GBPUSD can both increase short-USD exposure. Choose one setup or divide the same total risk budget; do not multiply it. The open-trade count is an additional cap, not permission to fill every slot.

Use the card's Entry Zone and actual Stop Loss. The 1.5R subscriber checkpoint is a breakeven-management reference when reachable within the actual plan; automated execution uses the account's assigned management rules. TP2 and Target R:R show the canonical target. Some strategies target less than 3R: do not extend those targets because of a runner label. Setup labels describe objectives, not promises of a same-day exit, a fixed holding time or a profitable outcome.

Do not chase a missed entry, add to a losing trade or revenge-trade after a stop. A VIP alert is an opportunity to assess, not an instruction to use every available trade slot. Respect your prop firm's tighter limits, market restrictions and news/holiday rules. Stops and daily limits cannot eliminate gaps or slippage.
