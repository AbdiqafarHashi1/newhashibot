## Customer and Admin authority

Customer trading routes show account policy, provider observations, positions, protection and outcomes through the existing safe account report. They do not expose risk, strategy, credential, pairing or runtime editors. Checkout, signal onboarding, support requests and risk acknowledgement remain available.

The Phase-H endpoint restricts customer actions, preserves Admin-on-behalf-of-customer scope and keeps Admin account policy/strategy/limit controls. MT5 account mutations and Phase-N operation commands require Admin. A forward migration guards trading configuration writes, including writes through security-definer browser RPCs. The legacy nullable-owner risk tables no longer expose shared rows to customers, and the legacy risk reservation function is service-only.

## Risk and lifecycle integration

- Prop entry authorization caps daily drawdown policy at 4%, retaining any stricter approved cap. The evaluation default also uses 4%; this cannot guarantee an observed loss never gaps beyond a threshold.
- Crypto, Gold and FX share a conservative USD concentration bucket. This sums risk without assuming opposite directions or different instruments hedge each other. Account scopes remain isolated.
- The legacy Python gateway no longer invents equity/approve when PostgreSQL account authority is unavailable. Its breaker halts unknown/invalid account observations and does not automatically reopen after a daily breach.
- Legacy internal netting cannot pretend to close provider exposure; Redis scope/locks include provider and account, and accepted intents no longer advance confirmed exposure.
- A legacy Friday liquidation helper cannot report success without a real native close; it excludes crypto and preserves reservations on unknown provider state. This helper is not a replacement for canonical provider trade management.
- Broker-specific minute session conventions and discovery-gap handling are retained, with bounded session caching; Gold RSI trailing and fixed-bracket TP1/breakeven handling coexist.
