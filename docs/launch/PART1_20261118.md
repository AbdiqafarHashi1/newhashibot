## House-Money authority

`house-money-risk.ts` is the shared pure policy consumed by account authorization, submission-time revalidation, the generic risk engine and the saved-ledger simulation. Thresholds follow the requested Personal, Challenge and Funded rules. Weekend risk is 0.75x. Venue contract, margin, concentration, explicit live permission and purchased Prop terms can further reduce or block an order.

`account_risk_state` persists `equity_phase`, `base_risk_pct`, `applied_risk_pct`, `block_reason` and the observed equity/period baselines. Source observations must be no older than 60 seconds. Monthly net profit comes from account-bound closed provider outcomes, with consistent account currency and complete closed execution coverage. The first monthly balance is reconstructed from observed balance minus that month's closed net; subsequent observations preserve it. Unexplained balance changes on a flat account require reconciliation, avoiding automatic attribution of deposits to profit. Floating equity affects daily/peak/total losses; unrealized gains cannot unlock the monthly-profit escalation.

Hard peak/total/phase locks persist. Daily locks last through the account's approved reset day; the Funded monthly target lock lasts through the UTC month. Phase 2 follows the existing `VERIFICATION` account setup. A new phase account retains its own ownership/baseline. Policy-state writes are service-only; customer views remain read-only.

The legacy SQL gateway uses fresh canonical risk state when account IDs are mapped, otherwise retaining its strict old caps. Its unavailable-authority fallback still denies entries. Python breaker registration accepts the explicit equity mode and uses 4%, 3.25% or 2.25% daily ceilings without automatic recovery from a breached latch.

## Friday management

From Friday 20:00 UTC until Sunday 21:00 UTC, FX/Metals entries are blocked at account authorization, queue creation, adoption and native MT5 execution. Eligible BTC/ETH/SOL USDT setups can continue at 0.75x risk, subject to current account truth. MT5 crypto CFD handling requires an explicit allowlist, fresh native contract and an already-qualified compatible strategy; the sixteen configurations do not fabricate an extra CFD strategy or translate perpetual orders into CFDs.

Fresh MT5 snapshots enqueue idempotent cancellation of owned non-crypto orders. Configured, explicitly weekend-restricted Prop accounts also enqueue owned position closes. Manual positions and other customers' tickets are excluded. Source permissions, current window, current snapshot and ownership are rechecked when the command is claimed. An expired/invalid close authorization cannot fall through into a normal mutation. Native agent protective authorization remains scoped to its supported demo terminal identity.
