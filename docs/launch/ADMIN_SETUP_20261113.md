# Admin setup and account controls

Open **Setup & controls** (`/admin/launch`). Four sections group the existing operational actions rather than introducing a second control system.

1. **Market feeds**: connect a dedicated MT5 system/admin account using investor credentials, execution disabled, and assign/pair its Windows bridge. The create link preselects system ownership, investor capability and demo classification. Choose it in the embedded feed form and use the same feed identity in the bridge and scanner. This supplies Forex and metals. The scanner uses public Binance klines for crypto; exchange trading API credentials are not required for those public candles.
2. **Admin demos**: onboard separate MT5 master-enabled demo, Binance testnet and Bybit demo accounts. The scanner's investor account is data-only. Admin-owned trading accounts are identified by the authenticated owner's ID and can be filtered independently from customer accounts in Providers. Each connection retains its own provider, environment, credentials, balance and controls.
3. **Customers**: personal onboarding and Prop phase selection, customer access/expiry, risk and permitted strategies. Prop supports up to $25,000 original USD capital. A successful $25k account can grow above $25k; the ceiling applies to the original observed baseline, not profit. Admin firm-rule approval, current provider evidence ingestion and production new-entry admission enforce the ceiling. Unknown/non-USD denominations cannot silently be treated as USD. Larger retrospective research accounts do not authorize larger customer execution. Position monitoring and exit services are unaffected.
4. **Telegram & 24h checks**: channel permissions/test controls, lifecycle and delivery recovery, schedule installation/control and a new fixed rolling-day review. Reads list exact counts plus bounded latest records for scanner runs, lifecycle observations and publication operations, with setup/event/destination identities and message-part uncertainty. A read failure displays unavailable, never a successful zero. Queued/attempted publication is not delivered proof. Tests send only when explicitly pressed in the existing channel controls.

No services, credentials, schedules, payments, channels or provider orders are activated by this UI update. Supabase must be restored and the current backend/frontend deployed. Then observe a full connected day: source freshness; watch and trigger confirmation; entries and targets/stops/expiry; Free/Crypto VIP/Forex-Gold VIP routing; Admin incidents; retries without duplicate delivery. The day screen is the place to inspect that evidence, not a substitute for the run.

FTMO allows EAs but its personal-use terms prohibit third-party account management. Confirm that the actual buyer-operated automation arrangement is permitted before connecting a firm's account; the product's $25k ceiling is HashiBot policy, not a general FTMO EA limit.

Public pages now describe the customer product and workflow without development-status banners or unconnected-example status tiles. Actual checkout/provider failures and truthful risk information remain in the flows. No operational authorization controls were removed.
