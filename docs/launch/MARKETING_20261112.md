# HashiBot first launch cohort

## Offer and position

Lead with a concrete service: conditional trading plans, confirmation when the market satisfies the condition, and tracked updates to the outcome. Explain signal-only membership separately from account execution. Sell a process and accountable delivery; never promise a monthly return or a prop pass. Use real completed signal lifecycles as acquisition content. The 5% / 10% and leveraged 10–50% numbers remain internal research targets, rather than subscription promises.

VIP: current $29 / KSh 3,500 monthly offer, using the configured Whop plan and M-Pesa Till collection. Execution: current $100/account/month offer with each customer's own provider, risk and exact strategy selection. Begin execution on demo/testnet as requested. Explain that first-month demo onboarding before a live account connection is part of this execution cohort.

## First two days after service activation

| Step | Deliverable | Conversion action | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 morning | One complete setup story: watch → condition fulfilled → entry plan → follow-up, with genuine timestamps | Join Free | Campaign link → signup → Telegram admission |
| Day 1 afternoon | Short screen recording of provider onboarding and per-customer risk/strategy controls | Request an execution demo setup | Lead → booked onboarding → connected demo account |
| Day 1 evening | Live product walkthrough to the owner's existing trading audience; answer conditional signal and demo execution questions | Choose VIP or execution | Checkout started → verified payment → correct access |
| Day 2 morning | Market briefing and sample conditional watch; no invented trade when conditions are absent | Join Free, then VIP | Free signups and retained Telegram members |
| Day 2 afternoon | Owner-led one-to-one onboarding for the first cohort | Connect demo account | Successful account read, risk selection, order/protection/closure evidence |
| Day 2 evening | Explain the day's actual updates, including invalidated conditions and closed results | VIP upgrade | Upgrades, support questions and incomplete checkouts |

Start with the owner's network and trading communities that permit promotions. Request permission from group owners; do not bulk message scraped contacts. Publish a short visual walkthrough rather than a wall of technical architecture. Optional paid promotion comes after the payment-to-access journey works and the first cohort's support load is understood; no advertising spend is authorized by this document.

Attach the actual Free or pricing link with source attribution. Do not announce a live scanner, active checkout or completed provider execution until those services have been connected and observed. No messages or campaigns have been sent by this implementation.

## Daily owner dashboard

Track unique landing visits, Free registrations, successful Telegram joins, VIP checkout starts, verified payments, paid memberships, execution onboarding starts, connected demo accounts, support cases and cancellations. Track content delivery failures separately from customer conversion. A small active cohort with working onboarding is more useful for launch than the long-term 1,000 Free / 1,000 VIP targets.
