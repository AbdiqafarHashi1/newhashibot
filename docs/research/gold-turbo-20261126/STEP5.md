# Work period 5: flagship Basket Scalper

Implemented as demo research modules; MT5/account authorization and actual provider execution remain period 6. Historical profitability remains periods 8-9. No production strategy/catalog or account was activated.

- `basket_strategy.py`: competes qualified entries from the four existing Turbo families plus explicit previous-candle anticipation and micro-exhaustion reversal. Logs selected source/model and rejected alternatives. Ranking is a geometry/regime heuristic, not a probability; close opposing scores reject instead of forcing unanimous agreement or contradictory entries.
- `basket.py`: freezes original equity/risk/margin/gross-exposure envelopes and reserves pending and filled exposure together. Multiple same-direction child reservations cannot increase the original allowance. Minimum executable volume is rejected when unaffordable; volume floors conservatively. Realized losses remain debited; winning closes do not reset the allowance.
- Net basket marks combine realized fills, unrealized executable-side quote value, accrued carry and estimated exit friction. Individual profitable-position closure and full basket targets/trailing/loss/invalidation closure are separate. The configurable 0.4% initial-equity target is tested as an experiment; default research target is 0.2%, not a performance claim.
- Bulk close/cancel actions are proposals. Partial fills, uncertain submissions and missing provider deals retain exposure and block unsafe resubmission. A basket is not closed while positions or unfilled reservations remain. Deal replay is idempotent; actual adverse fills are retained and flagged, not discarded to preserve simulated risk compliance.
- Optional hedging requires demo Personal hedging mode, explicit temporary exposure-reduction purpose, cost budget, gross ratio cap and a fixed non-renewing duration. Hedge stop-risk is counted without offsetting base risk. Duration/purpose ending requests hedge closure; it cannot postpone the basket's loss stop indefinitely.
- Position/action ownership is basket-scoped. Netting with unrelated XAU exposure is blocked; multi-order netting scaling remains blocked until a verified adapter can reconcile shared tickets. No assumed broker compatibility.
- JSON checkpoint/export retains reservations, positions, deal identities, marks and events. Restore enters recovery and makes unresolved actions uncertain. Fresh scoped position quantity/stop and pending-order reconciliation is required before resuming. Atomic disk persistence, authenticated provider observations, broker-side protection installation, disconnect scheduling and account-wide daily/total loss computation are still period 6 work.

BrokerFacts and BasketEnvelope are explicit research inputs, not proof of a real authorization. Contract size, quote/account conversion and per-lot margin must be supplied; actual nonlinear broker margin and actual fills need period 6 verification. Default nonexperimental basket allowance cannot exceed 2% of initial equity; this is not a substitute for the separate 1-2% daily account loss guard. Operational position caps default to none; a configurable order-operation limit protects memory. The period 6 journal writer must persist audit events progressively rather than rely on an indefinitely growing in-memory book.

Verification: targeted Gold Turbo tests and Python compilation pass. Synthetic tests cover competing models, long/short selection, reservation precision, shared risk, pending uncertainty, margin/minimum lots, netted isolation, adverse fills, combined P&L, targets/trailing, partial bulk closure, hedge constraints and checkpoint recovery. No historical backtest, data acquisition, full-repository test, live order or paid activation occurred.
