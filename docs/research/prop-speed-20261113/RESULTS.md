# Prop speed, edge and commercial decision

2026-11-13. This study investigated whether the earlier slow results were caused by excessive protection, weak strategies, low participation, position sizing or phase management. It does not activate a customer or guarantee a profit, pass or payout.

**Two-week passing is possible in this history: one 10/5 scenario funded in 13.8 days. Five-percent months are also possible: the normal bundle exceeded 5% in May and June. Neither result became a dependable customer outcome across later starts.**

| --- | --- | --- |

The strongest two-week example was the full reviewed bundle, 2% research risk, ceiling 10, 3R exits and at most 24 native strategy bars: **13.816 days** for 10/5 from a March 29 start. Only one of twelve earlier starts of that variant achieved two-week funding; no selected later challenge variant did. The exact fastest unbanked variant was additionally checked on all five later starts: 0/5 within 14 days and 2/5 within 30 days, completing in 16.04 and 23.39 days, with no modeled failures. Generic 8/5's fastest earlier case was 17.25 days. Different minimum-day and holding profiles mean these minima do not show that a larger target is inherently easier.

Challenge replays continue after funding until their date boundary at the same experimental funded risk; failure counts cover the entire window. One earlier 2% full-bundle case passed and subsequently failed after funding. This is retained rather than presented as successful account survival. None of the newly selected later banking cases both passed and then failed; their recorded failures were separate unsuccessful cases.

These larger sizing/exit variants remain research, not customer policy changes. Raising size does not repair the nine-loss sequence. Higher-water loss protection is stricter than a static firm loss floor, but changing to a buffered static model did not solve the later-period pass problem.

## Profit banking: a useful missing behavior

Previously the historical account could keep using full risk after already reaching a phase's profit requirement while waiting for its minimum entry days. The new isolated banking study realizes an executable profit buffer before all days are complete, then uses at most 0.10% on subsequent genuine qualified entries and less when the remaining buffer is small. It skips another day-completion entry on a day already counted. It does not invent trades, add unqualified token orders, declare days that have not occurred or waive firm rules. It resets phase state only after the normal verified phase transition.

The 1.5% speed selection improved from 0/5 to 2/5 later one-month passes. Three simultaneous positions were tested as a separate follow-up and did not provide a consistent solution. The banking logic here is historical-account research; current customer terminal automation is not represented as having this phase-banking feature.

## Are scanner gates suppressing a good system?

A complete **43,200-minute** canonical probe compared the same first 30 days (March 1–31). The relaxed research admission profile removes cooldown/hourly/global-track constraints while retaining shared entry evaluation, confirmation, opposing-exposure handling and account risk admission.

Confirmations increased **43 → 63 (+46.5%)**, but at normal sizing customer entries increased only **35 → 36**. Marked account return improved **-5.46% → -3.92%**, still missing both the pass and funded-profit goals. These numbers are marked results over the matched 30-day period, so they differ from the calendar-month closed-cash table above. Three-position and larger-size comparisons are retained in the evidence.

This probe is not a completed six-month relaxed replay. The broader continuous probe was stopped and replaced by the bounded matched-month experiment to control local computation. No incomplete output is counted as a completed scenario. Raw opportunities rejected while an existing watch or trade remains active are not independent lost winning trades.

## New high-frequency / lower-reward exploration

An additional **2,592 short-hold configurations / 5,188 approximate period cases** tested Gold, EURUSD and USDJPY; RSI2, band re-entry, failed breakout and squeeze; 5m/15m; 0.5R, 0.75R, 1R and 1.5R targets; and short 3/6/12-bar holding periods. Commission was $5.25 per lot per side and slippage 0.75bps, with native bid/ask, lot steps and the screen's explicit carry reserve.

Four Gold finalists were selected from positive earlier periods with participation and drawdown conditions. All four lost money in May–August; EURUSD and USDJPY had no shortlist meeting the earlier conditions. Two Gold examples raised win rates above 51% but still had later profit factors below one. They were rejected without spending another full canonical replay on failed candidates. This screen is not a Prop Brain or firm-rule qualification.

## Repeated rewards, not isolated target hits

Sixty recurring campaigns tested Gold range / both configurations, 2% / 5% goals, risk reduction after 2% loss, five March–July starting dates, both loss bases and one/three-day processing/new-account handoffs. Each completed cycle waits at least 14 days from its first trade, removes gross profit, assumes an 80% reward share, and resumes only after a modeled new account issuance. This is not permission to clear a running customer's drawdown ledger.

For the higher-risk Gold-both 2% candidate with a three-day handoff, the March campaign modeled six completed cycles and $2,129 remaining after six assumed $100 monthly fees. Its July and August reward months were both **zero**. A new July-start campaign modeled three cycles and $1,042 remaining over two months. The starting state and missed trading opportunities change later results; neither isolated 80% fortnight counts nor restarting the ledger can be treated as dependable recurring income.

The 5% candidate's March campaign modeled four cycles and $4,330 after fees, concentrated before June; the July-start campaign produced no completed reward and -$200 after assumed fees. All zero months and incomplete final cycles remain in the admin report and archive. Taxes, VPS, challenge purchases, firm approval and actual payment processing are not verified cash flows.

## Commercial decision

The goals are possible; the current edge does not make fast challenge completion reliable. Offer the execution/management function with an explicit profit target, account risk controls and observed results. Do not turn selected best-case pass times into a promised delivery deadline or a fixed monthly return. The stronger next demonstration is an already-funded account with a defined 2% or 5% cutoff, separately from an evaluation-speed product.

Today's hosted prerequisite remains concrete: **Supabase project uqiwbzfcntlcyyewawfc was observed INACTIVE**. This commit is prepared in the existing draft PR, not deployed. Immediate customer autotrading is not established by local tests. Account onboarding/demo bookings can be offered truthfully while the intended backend, current functions/frontend/agent and owner MT5 demo are activated and observed end to end. Subscription-expiry full closure remains a separate earlier operational gap; this study does not mark paid live execution ready.
