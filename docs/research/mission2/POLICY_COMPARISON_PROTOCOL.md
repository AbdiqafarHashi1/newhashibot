# Chronological policy comparison protocol

All three entry policies use the same ten-symbol universe, account Brain and cost
assumptions within each cohort. The windows have already been observed; they are
not new untouched holdouts. This comparison is diagnostic, not an optimization or
production promotion.

The CI continuation includes earlier monthly candles so a 4h strategy has at
least 240 closed bars before the research start:
- February 17–24, 2025: January and February archives.
- March 17–24, 2025: January through March archives.
- October 20–27, 2026: September and October archives.

The original single-month screens did not provide adequate 4h or daily warm-up.
Their missing-history rejections must not be interpreted as negative strategy
profitability or evidence that the production entry engine is too conservative.
These earlier screens remain labeled provisional; the expanded-warm-up cohorts
are distinct experiments. Daily strategies requiring longer history remain
INSUFFICIENT DATA, not rejected.

Binance and Bybit use their actual account policy modules, but both are exposed
to the same genuine Binance spot historical price stream in this controlled
comparison. This is a cross-provider policy counterfactual, not certification of
historical Bybit liquidity or fills.

Jobs persist restart checkpoints, source hashes, data manifests, frozen signal
snapshots, equity paths and full funnel observations as CI artifacts. Failure,
cancellation and incomplete data must remain visible. No live trading credentials,
orders, strategy releases, or deployment are involved.
