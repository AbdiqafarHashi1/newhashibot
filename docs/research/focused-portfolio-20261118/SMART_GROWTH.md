# Smart Growth follow-up to PR #276

Standard remains the default for new Personal execution accounts: 1% base with the existing dynamic House-Money escalation/throttling, 6% open exposure and 18% peak lock. No customer is automatically upgraded.

Smart Growth is an optional Admin assignment: 1.5% base, 7% open cap, 4.5% factor cap, 6% daily entry lock and 14% peak entry lock. Crypto weekend risk is multiplied by 0.75. Second and later concurrent Crypto entries receive another 0.50 multiplier before exposure and margin checks. Growth 2% retains PR #276's limits; its correlation guard is an explicit Admin option. Old stored GROWTH_2 / AGGRESSIVE_5 names remain readable aliases for GROWTH_2PCT / AGGRESSIVE_5PCT.

Upgrade eligibility is checked in Admin assignment, production authorization, and SQL persistence. The eligibility RPC reads immutable provider observations and reconciled closed outcomes belonging to that exact customer/account/provider/environment. Thirty elapsed days must have passed since BOTH the first verified provider observation and first verified closed provider trade. Account creation date and backtest records do not qualify. All recorded canonical closes require provider outcomes. Eligibility is rechecked against the database within 60 seconds; actual order authorization separately requires current provider context and equity no older than 60 seconds. Assignment can be saved while trading is paused; it does not create fresh financial observations. No trade is required on each quiet/weekend day. This establishes the age and completeness of recorded canonical forward history, not continuous tick coverage or a profitability guarantee.

Smart Growth can be assigned on demo/testnet or live accounts that meet the history requirement. Existing live permissions, entitlement gates, provider restrictions and tighter risk ceilings still apply; this change does not grant live execution or raise the existing bounded live permission ceiling. The 2%/Aggressive profiles retain their sandbox restrictions. Experimental assignment is Admin-only, and the public guide contains no aggressive sizing scenario. Customer trading remains view-only.

Recommended Personal execution capital is $2,000+ for the $100/month service. Admin operations, customer account reports and onboarding show a suitability notice, including a warning below $1,500 in observed USD/USDT/USDC equity. Unknown currencies require observed conversion rather than a numeric comparison with USD. This is an advisory warning, not a new deposit lock. Firm challenge sizes of $25k–$100k are distinguished from HashiBot's current $25k automated Prop cap.

Personal comparisons use the five-engine selection from #276, split into provider/market accounts with fully covered data. All policy rows use the same hypothetical entry-margin budget for comparison; Standard therefore has an added explicit research margin assumption. Guard tests include both Smart Growth and Growth 2% on the two Crypto windows. Open positions can overshoot an entry lock before their source exit; the lock does not invent a forced fill. Existing automatic Crypto evidence currently requires a flat account before another entry, so actual demo concurrency may be lower than this saved-ledger experiment. The guard does not bypass that requirement.

VIP comparisons use one global entry slot per UTC day across both markets and all fully covered members of the eight-source allowlist. Coverage is 7/8, 4/8, 7/8 and 2/8 in the inherited July 2025, September 2026, June 2025 and July 2026 windows respectively. The strict short 2026 VIP window is slightly negative; it is retained. Source management, costs and same-symbol locks remain; discretionary subscriber breakeven changes are not modeled. All results reuse earlier research history and measure realized-close drawdown, excluding floating loss and liquidation. Trading returns exclude subscription fees, tax and withdrawals. These are interval returns, not monthly forecasts.
