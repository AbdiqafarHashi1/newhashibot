The catalog uses the existing evaluator functions. It does not put different strategy names on a generic trigger. Enabled settings and user allowlists are respected. Forex/Gold symbol contracts cannot be widened through arbitrary configuration. Only the former exact ForexMicroScalper default 15m allowlist is expanded to 5m/15m; custom lists and disabled controls are preserved.

Each admissible named result keeps its version, configuration, complete original StrategyPlan, original stop/targets, evidence, cost model and an explicit future confirmation contract. Stops/targets are not regenerated by AI. The conditional trigger is the appropriate original entry-zone boundary. The future completed candle must confirm, followed by the next observed open within the frozen chase limit. Expiry uses evaluator-provided timeout bars when present; holding uses evaluator-reported bars when present. Original raw plan and applied paper rules are both retained.

The paper model is explicit: original static stop, 50% TP1 / remainder TP2, or one full target when the originating levels coincide; stop first for ambiguous closed candles. No extra break-even or trailing behavior is invented for a strategy whose contract lacks it. Actual provider/account execution is separate.

## What makes the decision contextual

Every named proposal records live-source candle identity, higher-timeframe direction, local trend, ATR/range, volume ratio, Forex session, current native spread when fresh, and available source intelligence. Crypto alts also record BTC context. Price history, higher context and historical callbacks cannot read future candles or future news snapshots. Quotes older than two minutes become unavailable rather than a current-spread claim. Disclosed modeled cost reserves continue to apply.

Trend strategies require their directional context; breakouts must retain their original boundary; reversal theses keep structural invalidation. Low volume, volatility shocks, opposing BTC context or a structured high-impact event window can hold confirmation. A changed context at the entry open cancels the pending paper entry. Once a position is active, original protection and paper management continue independently of news or discovery toggles.

News is source evidence, not a probability score. Headline text never supplies an invented future event time. Supported calendar evidence is a structured `high_impact_events` array of `{currency, impact: "HIGH", at: ISO8601-with-timezone, source_url: HTTPS}` inside a stored intelligence event. The configured Forex news provider can populate it. Unavailable calendar/news is recorded as unavailable; it is never described as a verified clear calendar. The strategy can still use its actual price/structure intelligence under the recorded analysis policy. No continuously verified economic-calendar feed is claimed merely because MT5 candles are connected.

## Coverage and publication

The 30-definition catalog includes the ten primary evaluators and 20 research definitions (regime, VWAP/failed auction, relative strength/weakness, sleeves, daily trend, cost-aware models, coordinated roles, and research pullback/reversal). Research definitions stay disabled/shadow unless their existing release controls authorize them. This change does not promote failed studies, create performance certification, or silently reinterpret a target-free native management contract as a fixed-target strategy. Such a contract is explicitly held rather than assigned invented targets.

`watched_strategy_observations` records the strategy/symbol/frame decision and source evidence, with administrator-only reads and service-only writes. Disabled, ineligible, no-setup, held-context and qualified-watch decisions remain distinct. Qualified proposals are not counted as sent trades; only a successful setup transaction creates canonical publication work. Observations deduplicate unchanged candle/configuration/decision evidence, and reject older overwrites. The coverage snapshot aggregates in SQL to avoid REST row-limit truncation.

Existing broad conditional profiles remain available, with their own identities and source-backed context. All subscriber publications still use atomic setup/history/outbox transactions, exact setup references, market-specific VIP destinations, and the preselected daily Free sample per market. Free keeps the sample's follow-ups and upgrade invitations. Technical failures remain Admin-only. Missing source candles or changed venues cannot become an assumed win or loss.

## Verification boundary

Synthetic candle fixtures and injected transports verify evaluator connections, source timing, state transitions, paper arithmetic, permissions and routing. They are not market-performance evidence. The deployment must still be rehearsed with the real connected MT5 feed and actual Telegram destinations before live service verification is recorded.
